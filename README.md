## ✨ Key Features

*   **Universal Multi-Platform Extraction:** Seamlessly download individual videos, audio tracks, and shorts from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and over a thousand other supported platforms.
*   **Audio-Only Extraction:** Instantly extract and convert media into high-quality audio files (like MP3 or M4A) perfect for podcasts, music libraries, or voice-overs.
*   **Subtitle & Metadata Embedding:** Automatically fetch and embed multi-language subtitles, thumbnails, and chapter markers directly into your downloaded media files for a complete offline experience.
*   **Accelerated Parallel Downloading:** Utilizes multi-threaded fragment downloading to bypass server-side throttling, maximizing your bandwidth for ultra-fast downloads.
*   **Restricted Content Access:** Seamlessly authenticate via browser cookies to download private, members-only, or age-restricted content you legally have access to.
*   **Playlist & Batch Processing:** Paste a playlist link to parse, search, and batch-download multiple videos into automatically generated folders.
*   **Advanced Selection UI:** Choose exact formats and presets for individual videos using a drag-to-select dialog with live search filtering.
*   **High-Resolution Support:** Automatically detects and downloads pristine 4K and 8K streams.
*   **Format Standardization:** Embedded FFmpeg post-processing forces automatic container remuxing into standard `.mp4` format for maximum device compatibility.
*   **Smart File Management:** Highlights previously downloaded videos to prevent duplicates and provides instant "Play" and "Show in Folder" actions upon completion.
*   **Seamless Auto-Updates:** A rebuilt native automatic updater safely bypasses SSL blocks and cleanly installs future patches without touching your registry uninstaller.

## 📥 Installation

1. Download the latest `MediaDoor_v1.2_setup.exe` from the [Releases](../../releases/latest) page.
2. Run the installer (it will cleanly install into your `Program Files` directory).
3. Launch MediaDoor from your Start Menu or Desktop shortcut.

## 💡 Known Issues

* **Extraction Errors:** Occasionally, the app may display a download or extraction error. This is a common issue related to the core `yt-dlp` engine whenever platforms (like YouTube) update their backend code. We actively monitor these upstream changes and push auto-updates to patch them as quickly as possible.

## ⚠️ Usage Disclaimer
By installing this software, you agree to only download content you have the legal right or permission to access. MediaDoor is intended strictly for personal, offline viewing.
